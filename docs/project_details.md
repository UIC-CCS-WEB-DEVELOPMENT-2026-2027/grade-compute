# CompUGrade

**Demystifying Academic Progress through Transparent Grade Tracking**

## Project Overview

CompUGrade is a centralized academic platform designed to eliminate the anxiety and confusion surrounding grading systems. Often, students are handed complex syllabi with varying multipliers, weights, and grading periods, leaving them in the dark about their actual academic standing until the end of the semester.

CompUGrade bridges this gap by translating complex syllabus rules into an interactive, user-friendly dashboard. It empowers students to track their official grades in real-time, understand exactly how their scores are computed, and strategically plan their academic efforts to reach their target final grades.

## Primary Goals

1. **Promote Transparency:** Ensure students have clear, continuous access to their academic standing without relying on manual computation or guessing.

2. **Empower Strategic Learning:** Provide interactive tools that allow students to simulate future grades, helping them prioritize their studying efforts based on what mathematically matters most.

3. **Standardize Grading Expectations:** Create a unified interface where the computation rules of different subjects and professors are applied accurately and automatically.

## Target Audience

* **Primary (Phase 1):** College and Graduate Students who need to actively manage complex grading formulas, predict their final outcomes, and stay organized across multiple advanced subjects.

* **Secondary (Future Phases):**

  * **High School and Elementary Students:** To help younger learners build early habits of academic responsibility, self-reflection, and goal setting in a simplified workspace.

  * **Parents and Guardians:** To provide a transparent window into their child's academic progress in real-time, allowing them to understand syllabus expectations and better support their educational journey.

  * **Professors and Faculty:** Who need a streamlined, transparent way to distribute grades and syllabus formulas directly to their classes and students' parents.

  * **School Counselors and Administrators:** To utilize macro-level data for identifying at-risk students early, facilitating timely academic interventions, and monitoring overall institutional health.

## Core Features

### 1. The Student Hub

A personalized, centralized dashboard where students can view all their currently enrolled subjects at a glance. It provides a clean, distraction-free environment that summarizes class information, professor details, and overall progress.

### 2. The 3-State Grading Engine

At the heart of the application is an intelligent grading workspace that categorizes academic tasks (like assignments, quizzes, and exams) into three distinct states:

* **Official (Locked):** Grades that have been finalized and verified by the professor. These cannot be altered by the student.

* **Self-Reported (Sandbox):** Unofficial scores inputted by the student for tasks they have completed but are waiting to get back.

* **Empty (Upcoming):** Future tasks that have not yet occurred.

### 3. The Target Grade Sandbox (Predictive Modeler)

Rather than just looking at the past, CompUGrade looks to the future. The Sandbox allows students to input a "Desired Final Grade" or plug hypothetical scores into upcoming exams and assignments. The system immediately calculates the math, showing students exactly what scores they need to achieve on future tasks to hit their target milestone.

### 4. Dynamic Syllabus Computation

Every class has different rules—some value exams at 50%, while others weigh projects heavier. CompUGrade inherently respects these unique "computation rules." The system automatically applies the correct effective multipliers behind the scenes, ensuring the math is always perfectly aligned with the educator's specific syllabus.

### 5. Historical Analytics (Visual Trends)

While the Sandbox predicts the future, CompUGrade also tracks the past. The platform includes simple, intuitive historical analytics, allowing students to visualize their semester-over-semester GPA growth and performance trends across different subject categories.

## Engaging User Experience (UX)

To ensure the platform feels supportive rather than stressful, CompUGrade utilizes several design principles aimed at reducing academic anxiety:

* **Personalized Aesthetics:** Students can assign custom emojis and accent colors to individual subjects (e.g., 💻 for Programming in blue, 🧬 for Biology in green). This makes the dashboard vibrant and visually intuitive.
* **Visual Progress Metaphors:** Progress bars display the student's current official grade as a solid color, their target grade as a vertical finish line, and the "Sandbox" inputted grades as a translucent "projected" fill. This makes closing the gap to their goal feel tangible.
* **Conversational Copywriting:** The Target Grade Sandbox delivers mathematical predictions using an encouraging, conversational tone (e.g., "You need an 85% on the Final to hit your target. You've got this!") instead of harsh, clinical data points.
* **Micro-Interactions & Milestones:** Satisfying visual feedback, such as subtle animations or a quick confetti burst, triggers when a student officially locks in a grade that meets or exceeds their target, providing a healthy dopamine boost.
* **Supportive Mascot Integration:** A friendly, non-intimidating app mascot appears during empty states (e.g., "No upcoming tasks! Time to relax.") or when delivering sandbox predictions, helping to neutralize the tension of grade tracking.

## Phase 1 Scope (Minimum Viable Product)

The initial launch (Phase 1) is strictly focused on the **Student Experience**. It operates as a standalone student environment to prove the core concept of the grading engine and the sandbox. During this phase, administrative, parent, and professor roles are locked, and the focus is entirely on perfecting the student-facing predictive tools and interface.

## Long-Term Vision (Phase 2 & Beyond)

The ultimate vision for CompUGrade is a fully collaborative academic ecosystem. Future iterations will introduce:

* **Professor Workspaces:** Allowing educators to create classes, set their own computation formulas, and push official grades directly to students.

* **Parental Portals & Alerts:** Linking parent accounts to student profiles for unified progress tracking. This will include automated email or SMS notifications summarizing weekly progress, alerting parents when critical official grades are posted, or flagging when a student's predicted grade drops below a designated threshold.

* **Study Group & Peer-Tutoring Suggestions:** An intelligent recommendation system that identifies when a student is struggling in a specific subject and connects them with relevant campus study groups or peer-tutors who excel in those areas.

* **Seamless Ecosystem Integrations:** Connecting CompUGrade directly with the workflows schools already rely on. This includes syncing assignments and rosters with Google Classroom and Microsoft Teams, and supporting bulk grade uploads and exports via Excel/CSV spreadsheets.

* **Institutional Integration & Official Data Exporting:** Expanding the platform to handle automated sectioning, deep campus-wide adoption, and the ability to generate verifiable academic reports and transcripts (complete with official institutional seals) for scholarships or admissions.
